In function ABI/source order `(x,qw,qz,scales,gidx,partial,...)`, SASS parameter slot `c[0][0x168]` holds `qw`, `c[0][0x170]` holds `qz`, and `c[0][0x178]` holds `scales` (confirmed by declaration/launch parameter order and SASS address/dataflow). The four U16 loads use base `R26` formed at 0x0550 from `c[0][0x178]`, i.e. scales; corresponding half values are converted by `HADD2.F32` at 0x06c0,0x0780,0x07c0,0x08b0 and consumed in FMUL/FFMA. These U16 instructions are **scale** loads, not Q4.
Actual Q4 `uint32_t` source loads are SASS `LDG.E.CONSTANT.SYS` at 0x04a0,0x04b0,0x04c0,0x04d0, addresses `R46+4`, `R46+12`, `R46`, `R46+8`. `R46` is derived from `c[0][0x168]` (`qw`) by the row/channel address chain; results R48/R49/R51/R43 are nibble-shifted/masked at 0x0570/0580/05b0–0600 then combined with zero-point and dequantized. They are four 32-bit source load operations, not 16-bit Q4 loads. This semantic mapping supersedes the erroneous Q4 label in the earlier bullet; raw report/SASS remains unchanged. Parameter alignment and transaction/sector efficiency remain to be evaluated for T009; no claim yet that an aligned uint4 source expression reduces actual traffic or latency.
