### SASS / bottleneck interpretation
- Exact c57 SASS confirms a serial ascending 128-K loop and dynamic per-K g_idx/zero/scale lookup; the selected CTA has a single warp. It emits a sequence of Q4 loads, nibble extraction, metadata loads/conversion, FMUL, then four predicated FFMA operations at `0x08e0–0x0920` updating four FP32 accumulators. Relevant loads: g_idx at `0x0450`; **Q4 qweight** as four 32-bit `LDG.E.CONSTANT.SYS` at `0x04a0–0x04d0`; **scale** as four 16-bit `LDG.E.U16.CONSTANT.SYS` at `0x0560/0x0590/0x05a0/0x06f0`; zero-point values are decoded from qzero path (`0x0530` and dependent shifts/ops); conditional per-row activation loads appear through `0x0830`. This corrects any contrary prior label in E042; exact source provenance and argument-slot mapping are recorded in `experiments/E042-g022-c57-source-sampling/results.md`. The full SASS retains loop branches. This confirms a dependent serial recurrence interleaved with global/constant loads, not an absence of FMA. NCU long-scoreboard counters support memory/dependency latency as material, but do not uniquely attribute it to g_idx vs Q4 vs scale/zero.
- Long-scoreboard is a substantial stall category (~28–33%); short-scoreboard is negligible (~0.03–0.04%); math-pipe throttle is ~10–12%; not-selected ~17–21%. This is consistent with memory/scoreboard dependency latency being material, but not by itself sufficient to say extra CTA warps can hide it. Active cycles are 91–95% of elapsed, while issued warp instructions per active SMSP cycle are ~0.63–0.71; the CTA has one warp, so issue opportunities per CTA are intrinsically limited. NCU’s counters do not distinguish exact wait source and the measured kernel includes four N outputs/thread plus many scalar metadata/Q4 operations.

### Complete-Linear comparison and timing controls

Production c57 returns FP16 directly. The reference time therefore includes an extra FP32 result allocation and separate cast. This path mismatch biases the comparison against the reference; retain raw values, but do not cite them as valid native-FP16 c57 ratios. No E040 rerun/correction of the raw timing was performed. One-time extension import is outside the timed call. ## INFERRED
- Hardware evidence shows exact c57’s one-warp CTA has substantial long-scoreboard dependency stalls with negligible short-scoreboard, no spills, and low eligible/issued warp instructions per active SM cycle. This supports memory/dependency latency as a material kernel constraint and makes CTA-internal K splitting a testable hypothesis; it does not prove low CTA residency/issue is the sole or primary complete-operator limit.
- The tested E040 warp-sliced implementation is PERFORMANCE-NO-GO: it passes strict numerics but uses REG96, 8/16KiB shared/CTA, barriers, and per-product FP64/TwoSum arithmetic; its M4 calls are about 3× slower than the non-native FP32+cast c57 harness reference. These arithmetic and path-boundary confounds mean E040 does not falsify pure same-arithmetic warp scheduling or N-tile parallelism, nor isolate one cause for the regression. It rejects this exact implementation; T007 addresses a distinct N-tile-only hypothesis.
- This is an **architecture-specific PERFORMANCE-NO-GO**, not evidence that all optimization of c57 is impossible. No conclusion is generalized to all layers, shapes or other GPU architectures.
