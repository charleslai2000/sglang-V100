### SASS / bottleneck interpretation
- Exact c57 SASS confirms a serial ascending 128-K loop and dynamic per-K g_idx/zero/scale lookup; the selected CTA has a single warp. It emits a sequence of packed loads, nibble extraction, metadata loads/conversion, FMUL, then four predicated FFMA operations at `0x08e0–0x0920` updating four FP32 accumulators. Relevant loads are g_idx at `0x0450`, qzero-related loads at `0x04a0–0x04d0`, scales at `0x0560–0x06f0`, and conditional per-row activation/metadata loads through `0x0830`; the full SASS file retains exact loop branches. This confirms a dependent serial recurrence interleaved with global/constant loads, not an absence of FMA. The NCU long-scoreboard counters support memory/dependency latency as material, but do not uniquely attribute it to g_idx vs Q4 vs scale/zero.
- Long-scoreboard is a substantial stall category (~28–33%); short-scoreboard is negligible (~0.03–0.04%); math-pipe throttle is ~10–12%; not-selected ~17–21%. This is consistent with memory/scoreboard dependency latency being material, but not by itself sufficient to say extra CTA warps can hide it. Active cycles are 91–95% of elapsed, while issue per active cycle is only ~0.002; the CTA has one warp, so issue opportunities per CTA are intrinsically limited. NCU’s counters do not distinguish exact wait source and the measured kernel includes four N outputs/thread plus many scalar metadata/Q4 operations.

## INFERRED
- Hardware evidence shows exact c57’s one-warp CTA has substantial long-scoreboard dependency stalls with negligible short-scoreboard, no spills, and low eligible/issued warp instructions per active SM cycle. This supports memory/dependency latency as a material kernel constraint and makes CTA-internal K splitting a testable hypothesis; it does not prove low CTA residency/issue is the sole or primary complete-operator limit.
- The authorized warp-sliced architecture is falsified as a useful optimization for these representative L43 cells: both variants pass strict numerics but consume higher shared memory and regress complete-call latency by 2.6–3.3× at M4. Thus it cannot meet >=10% operation gate, much less justify the ≥5% model/service gates. Likely causes include added CTA barriers/shared handoff, no additional CTA count or global parallelism, and arithmetic/data-layout behavior; no single cause is isolated.
- This is an **architecture-specific PERFORMANCE-NO-GO**, not evidence that all optimization of c57 is impossible. No conclusion is generalized to all layers, shapes or other GPU architectures.

## Bounded answer
**PERFORMANCE-NO-GO for CTA-internal 2-/4-warp K slicing under T006.** Exact c57 fixture-only application replay ran safely with 4GiB process-tree cap and remained far above MemAvailable floor; counters show substantial long-scoreboard (~28–33%) and low short-scoreboard (~0.03–0.04%) at the selected kernel, but do not prove additional CTA warps can cure that stall. Both isolated candidates preserve the required layouts/interfaces and pass tested strict numerical checks, yet complete L43 M4 gate_up/down Graph Linear calls are 2.69–3.34× exact c57, with Eager 2.57–3.28× slower. This decisively fails the operator gate; no model/service stage or production change.
