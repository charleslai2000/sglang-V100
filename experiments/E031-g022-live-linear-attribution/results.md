## Bounded answer
Direct source-span instrumentation on real eager SGLang Decode confirms 44 calls each per Forward to Falcon's GPTQ gate_up/down, attention QKV and o_proj, Mamba in_proj/out_proj. At c4, the observed CUTLASS WMMA `[8,12,4]` geometry belongs to Mamba out_proj; `[8,8,4]` belongs to attention QKV; `[8,53,2]` belongs to Mamba in_proj. c4 Other GEMM therefore comprises specific attention and Mamba projections rather than an unattributed class. GPTQ remains the largest source-attributed time owner in measured c1/c2/c4 ranges and the already accepted Candidate is the only qualified optimization; the NCU counters, timing and service performance for a *new* source change remain to be collected.
