## INFERRED
- The complete-call result disqualifies the current implementation from integration; isolated mathematical correctness cannot compensate for ~13× net regression. No production Forward improvement, service TPS or TTFT is established or implied.
- NCU confirms WMMA instructions really execute. The current realization serially processes 24 or 96 groups inside every output CTA and materializes/reloads a shared FP32 tile per group; the enormous measured DRAM traffic and ~2.8ms single kernel, compared with ~2.4ms complete prototype call, identify this per-group output-tile traffic/serialization as the decisive algorithmic bottleneck. No arbitrary geometry sweep is warranted.
- Retain group-major mapping and numerical results only as reference; a materially different group-parallel ownership/reduction is an architectural new design and requires explicit next-stage scope before implementation.
